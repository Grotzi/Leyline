## Matchups
### Mono Green
In: 2x Burst Lightning, 1x Fire Magic or Pyroclasm (otd), 1x Magic Damper  
Optional: 1x Into the Floodmaw (otd), 1x Pym Particles  
Out: 2-4x Pompous Battlemage (otd), 1x Bulk Up

Strongly favored matchup, as they lack efficient and instant speed interaction. We should secure game 1. We expect them to board in removal (Meltstrider's Resolve) and possibly Mossborn Hydra, sometimes Leatherhead for Leyline and Sapling Nursery. There are 3 things that can go wrong, especially otd: (1) Our hand is slow and they are faster than us, (2) they remove our creatures and (3) they build up enough blockers (potentially with reach) to stall the game and prevent us from going for lethal.

The best card to board in is Burst, because of its low opportunity cost and high ceiling (bolting Elves and setting them back). Sweepers have a high ceiling when they 2for1, but can are terrible top deck later into the game. Firemagic has the upside of being able to kill a Mossborn Hydra or Chocobo after it enters for 1 and being cast on the opponents turn. Pyroclasm is more efficient at sweeping for 2. Board in Pym if they have blockers for flying. Board in Into the Floodmaw if they have a critical mass of landfall doubling effects like Mightform Harmonizer and Mossborn Hydra (denies otk and resets counters). Into the Floodmaw used to be much better when Cub was legal, it also works as a counter to Meltstrider's Resolve or Bushwack, but is more situational and higher opportunity cost than Burst.
The reasoning for boarding out Battlemage otd (and previously Hired Claw) is, that they often cannot attack into the opposing blockers. They will frequently have a big enough Chocobo or Surrak on T2, which would force us to already use pump spells if we wanted to attack. The most common winning lines are through Slickshot or an unblockable Elusive Otter (potentially with fling) or by generating a few Drake Tokens.

### Orzhov Lifegain
