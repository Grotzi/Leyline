# Sideboard Guide
## General Notes
This is based of notes and ~150 matches from August-September, just before FRA.  
Hopefully this will get updated with the meta reshaping, but a lot of the insights from the last months should still apply.

### Overboarding
Leyline is a fragile deck that relies on obtaining a mix of lands, creatures, pump spells and damage doublers (Leyline, Fling, Bulk Up). The deck is too fast to consider filtering and draw. Therefore a every bit of the deck needs to optimized in order to maximize the chances of having just the right lethal and resilient composition of those card types in the opening hand and the first few turns.  
When sideboarding, every card that we remove from the maindeck decreases the chances of a such a mix coming together significantly. Also this deck is one of the if not the fastest in the current meta and should be able to go under most opposing strategies. For those reasons attack is generally our best defense, and we prefer sideboard cards that synergize well with the main strategy (for example a card like Dreadmaw's Ire that also happens to be a pump spell), cards with low CMC and cards that don't hurt the decks consistency.

### What to board out
The mainboard is designed to be as fast as possible game 1, assuming the opponent is not prepared and has not yet boarded in all their cheap interaction. In game 2 and 3 we will not get away so easily, which usually means bringing in more protection or interaction in place of gas.  
We can always board out 1x Bulk Up and 1x Reckless Ransacking. The mainboard Secret Identities are meant to be flexible, they can be protection if needed but can also allow for unblockable attacks or serve as a slightly worse pump spell. Postboard we can often replace 1-2 of those for a more precise answer. On the play and when having to go fast (like vs control) it may also be fine to board cut 1-2x Ancestral Anger.

## Matchups
### Mono Green
|   |   |
|---|---|
|**In**       |2x Burst Lightning, 1x Fire Magic or Pyroclasm (otd), 1x Magic Damper  |
|**Optional** |1x Into the Floodmaw (otd), 1x Pym Particles  |
|**Out**      |2-4x Pompous Battlemage (otd), 1x Bulk Up|


Strongly favored matchup, as they lack efficient and instant speed interaction. We should secure game 1. We expect them to board in removal (Meltstrider's Resolve) and possibly Mossborn Hydra, sometimes Leatherhead for Leyline and Sapling Nursery. There are 3 things that can go wrong, especially otd: (1) Our hand is slow and they are faster than us, (2) they remove our creatures and (3) they build up enough blockers (potentially with reach) to stall the game and prevent us from going for lethal.

The best card to board in is Burst, because of its low opportunity cost and high ceiling (bolting Elves and setting them back). Sweepers have a high ceiling when they 2for1, but can are terrible top deck later into the game. Firemagic has the upside of being able to kill a Mossborn Hydra or Chocobo after it enters for 1 and being cast on the opponents turn. Pyroclasm is more efficient at sweeping for 2. Board in Pym if they have blockers for flying. Board in Into the Floodmaw if they have a critical mass of landfall doubling effects like Mightform Harmonizer and Mossborn Hydra (denies otk and resets counters). Into the Floodmaw used to be much better when Cub was legal, it also works as a counter to Meltstrider's Resolve or Bushwack, but is more situational and higher opportunity cost than Burst.
The reasoning for boarding out Battlemage otd (and previously Hired Claw) is, that they often cannot attack into the opposing blockers. They will frequently have a big enough Chocobo or Surrak on T2, which would force us to already use pump spells if we wanted to attack. The most common winning lines are through Slickshot or an unblockable Elusive Otter (potentially with fling) or by generating a few Drake Tokens.

### Dimir Midrange
|   |   |
|---|---|
|**In**| 2x Magic Damper, 1x Fire Magic 1-2x Burst Lightning  |
|**Optional**| 1-2x Spell Pierce, 1x Pym Particles  |
|**Out** |1x Bulk Up, 1x Reckless Ransacking, 1-2 Secret Identity|

One of the harder matchups. They have great chump blockers, flying blockers, lots of cheap interaction. Worst of all the interaction comes in all shapes and forms (creatures, noncreatures, spot removal, counters, board wipes), making it hard to play around a certain type of spell. A frequent situation I ran into is playing around spot removal by keeping one mana up for protection with a single creature - to then get hit by a Strategic Betrayal. This makes sideboarding a lot less impactful, since we have to prepare for attacks from different angles.  
Dimir rarely taps out and has good answers for the Leyline gameplan, so we can't play too greedily. At the same time they will win the long game. So it comes down to finding the right window for an attack and often results into a stack of multiple answers and protection spells. Qarsi Revenant, Flashfreeze and Spell Snare are some of the many great cards they can board against us. Duress is quite dangerous and can disassemble a great hand into a poor one, but many players don't consider this card against Leyline (don't tell them).  
Magic Damper protects against spot removal and the flash creatures targeting, it also allows a surprise attack from a tapped down creature. Burst Lightning gets rid of Wasp, Tidebinder or maybe just a Siren in response to them cracking their map token.  
With Spell Pierce im not sure. It is the only way to for example protect a Slickshot from Flashfreeze or Snare, but it risks being a dead card at times. I would board depending on the cards we're seing in game 1, since there are slightly different builds of Dimir Midrange.  
Fire Magic used to be amazing in a similiar role as Burst Lightning, but with Deep-Cavern Bat falling out of fashion, it depends on how many 1-toughness targets it can still hit.  
Pym Particles can replace Ancestral Anger, but the toughness of their creatures is so low that it probably barely matters.  
Secret Identity is a lot worse in this matchup due to opposing flyers, so they get replaced by other protection spells.
